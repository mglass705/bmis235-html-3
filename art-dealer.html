PRODUCER PROFILE / NOT A RANKING

Art Dealer

Art Dealer (Jung Yean Cho) is a South Korean record producer, creative director, and designer best known for shaping the sound and visual identity of Playboi Carti and his Opium collective.

Art Dealer’s beats stand out in the underground scene for pairing heavy, distorted trap percussion with dreamlike, soaring electronic loops:

Playboi Carti: Produced the iconic Die Lit opener "Long Time (Intro)" along with seven tracks on Whole Lotta Red, including "Sky," "Over," "Control," "King Vamp," "Meh," "Die4Guy," and "Not PLaying".

Favorite production moments

Art Dealer's most recent recognitions have been his work in one of Carti's snippets that surfaced in 2025, "The Truth Is" (working title), and has also began implementing his own producer tag in some of Ken Carson's newest songs like "The Truth" and "swag jack".

What to listen for

Opium & Underground Collaborators: Crafted tracks for Opium signees Ken Carson ("Butterfly," plus work on X and The Xperiment) and Homixide Gang ("Lifestyle"), as well as underground staples like Yung Lean ("Trip").
