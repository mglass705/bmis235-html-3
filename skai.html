PRODUCER PROFILE / NOT A RANKING

Skai

skai is a Canadian record producer from Vancouver, British Columbia.

Why Skai stands out to me

Skai's production is not only very recognizable instantly, but it stands as to why his fans and listeners always look for his sound in music he creates. He may use a simliar 808 pattern in most beats, but that in pairing with his high-pitched etheral melodies is what stands out the most.

He is best known for his work with Playboi Carti, Ken Carson, Destroy Lonely, Nettspend, OsamaSon, and Che, among others.

What to listen for

Skai's drums are very drawn-out, and elaborate enough with an emotional-sounding melody, as we will soon hopefully see in OsamaSon's newest LP, Nocturnal. In that, he has a snippet where Skai produces called "Love Story", and the remakes and surfaced pieces of the beat are truly like no other.
